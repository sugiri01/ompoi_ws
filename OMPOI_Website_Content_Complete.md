|Understanding Price Spreads in Cashew Supply Chains|
|Sudhir G|
|November 05, 2025|
|Market Intelligence|
|12 min read|
|blog-cashew-spreads-hero.jpg|
||
|Cashew pricing in India varies dramatically across regions, quality grades, and seasons. Understanding these price spreads is essential for both buyers and sellers to make informed decisions and negotiate fair deals.|
||
|What Drives Price Spreads in Cashew Markets?|
|Price spreads in cashew supply chains are influenced by multiple factors including regional production variations, quality grading systems, processing costs, transportation logistics, seasonal demand patterns, and export market dynamics.|
|In Kerala, where cashew processing is concentrated, prices tend to be higher due to better infrastructure and proximity to export hubs. In contrast, tribal areas in Jharkhand and Odisha often see lower farmgate prices despite growing quality raw cashews.|
||
|The Role of Quality Grading|
|Cashew kernels are graded based on size, color, and wholeness. W180 (largest whole) commands premium prices, while W320 and W450 (smaller grades) are more affordable. Split and broken grades sell at significant discounts.|
|Quality-adjusted pricing is critical. A W180 kernel might cost ₹800-900/kg while W450 ranges ₹600-700/kg. Without standardized grading, buyers and sellers struggle to find fair price points.|
||
|How Data Transparency Helps|
|OMPOI's pricing intelligence provides visibility into market rates across regions and quality grades. By aggregating transaction data, we help buyers identify fair prices and sellers price competitively.|
|Our platform shows historical price bands, seasonal patterns, and regional variations — enabling both parties to negotiate from informed positions rather than guesswork.|
||
|Data consistency drives price discovery. When both buyers and sellers have access to market intelligence, transactions become faster, fairer, and more efficient.|
||
|Practical Recommendations|
|Always specify quality grade clearly in RFQs to get accurate quotes|
|Compare prices across multiple suppliers and regions before committing|
|Use historical data to understand seasonal price fluctuations|
|Factor in logistics and processing costs when comparing prices|
|Leverage platforms like OMPOI that provide pricing transparency|
||


|Understanding price spreads in cashew supply chains requires knowledge of regional dynamics, quality standards, and market trends. With better data and transparency, both buyers and sellers can achieve fair, profitable transactions while building long-term relationships based on trust.|

|What documents are required for verification?|
|Required documents include: GST certificate, PAN card, business registration certificate (for companies), bank account details, and address proof. Additional documents may be requested based on your business type and transaction volume.|
|Is there a fee to join OMPOI?|
|Registration is free for all users. We charge a small commission on successful transactions (2-3% for buyers, 1-2% for sellers) to maintain platform operations and provide ongoing support.|
||
||
|How does OMPOI determine pricing?|
|OMPOI doesn't set prices — sellers quote their own prices based on quality, quantity, location, and market conditions. Our pricing intelligence tool provides market data and trends to help both buyers and sellers make informed decisions.|
|Can I negotiate prices on the platform?|
|Yes, our platform includes a secure messaging system for price negotiations between buyers and sellers. All communication is logged for transparency and dispute resolution.|
|Are prices inclusive of taxes and shipping?|
|Prices are typically quoted FOB (Free on Board) or Ex-Works unless otherwise specified. Sellers should clearly mention if GST, transportation, or other charges are included. Always verify pricing terms before confirming orders.|
||
||
|Does OMPOI handle logistics and shipping?|
|We coordinate with third-party logistics partners and provide real-time tracking. Sellers can choose to arrange their own logistics or use our integrated network. All shipments are tracked through our platform for visibility and accountability.|
|What if there's a delay in delivery?|
|Our platform monitors delivery timelines and sends alerts for potential delays. In case of delays, both parties are notified immediately. Sellers must provide valid reasons for delays, and buyers can file complaints if SLAs are not met.|
|How is product quality ensured during transit?|
|We recommend appropriate packaging standards based on commodity type. For sensitive products, we offer temperature-controlled logistics partners. Quality issues upon delivery can be reported through our dispute resolution system.|

|What payment methods are supported?|
|We support bank transfers (RTGS/NEFT), UPI for smaller transactions, and digital wallets. For high-value orders, we offer escrow services where payment is held securely until delivery confirmation.|
|When is payment released to the seller?|
|For standard orders, payment is released upon delivery confirmation by the buyer. For escrow transactions, payment is released after quality inspection and buyer approval (typically 2-5 days post-delivery).|
|What if there's a payment dispute?|
|Our support team mediates payment disputes with complete transaction records. If quality issues are verified, buyers can request partial or full refunds. Sellers are protected against false claims through documentation and quality certificates.|
